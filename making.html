About Aram Se

आराम से — take it easy.

Aram Se is my making practice — a name that carries the Hindi phrase for "take it easy, take your time." Every piece is designed and sewn by hand, made to be used hard in the outdoors.

I work primarily with technical fabrics like Challenge Ultra, and EcoPack — materials that are incredibly strong, light, and weather-resistant. Each project is an exercise in thoughtful design and careful construction.
