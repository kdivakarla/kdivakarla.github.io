About

Making things with hands and head.

I'm Krishna — a maker and Earth scientist based in Colorado. Under the name Aram Se (आराम से — "take it easy" in Hindi), I design and sew ultralight outdoor gear: backpacks, sleeping quilts, handlebar bags, and more.

My scientific work focuses on geohazards in mountain environments — understanding how landslides, glacial change, and their interactions will shape future geographies.
